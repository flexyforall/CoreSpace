Our role

Core Space works alongside operators and platform builders, providing the critical systems they need to deploy, scale and operate in orbit.

Designed to scale

We are building a technology platform that can support the evolution of orbital infrastructure—from early deployments to large-scale data centers in space.

Built for orbit

Our systems are designed around the unique requirements of space: limited mass, extreme environments, continuous operation and the growing energy demands of orbital computing.

Power is the foundation

Orbital data centers require a fundamentally different approach to energy. We are developing the technology that can provide the power needed to operate them at scale.
